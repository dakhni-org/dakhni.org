About the Project

AI Policy

How artificial intelligence is used to compile and synthesise the content of this archive

Home›AI Policy

The information on this website is compiled and synthesised with the assistance of artificial intelligence, drawing from publicly available historical records, academic publications, and cultural archives. While every effort is made to ensure accuracy, AI-generated content may occasionally reflect interpretations rather than universally established fact.

I · How It's Used Compilation, Not Invention

Drawing from the historical record

Publicly available sources, brought together in one voice

Each page on Dakhni.org is compiled from publicly available historical records, academic publications, and cultural archives, and synthesised with the assistance of artificial intelligence into a single, consistent voice. The aim is to gather scattered scholarship on the Deccan into one accessible place, not to introduce new historical claims of its own.

II · What This Means for Readers Interpretation, Not Certainty

Accuracy is the goal, not a guarantee

AI-assisted synthesis can occasionally reflect interpretation rather than fact

While every effort is made to ensure accuracy, AI-generated content may occasionally reflect interpretations rather than universally established fact. Readers doing scholarly or historical research are encouraged to consult primary sources rather than treat any single page here as final.

III · Independence An Independent Project

Not affiliated with any institution

A personal cultural project, not an official record

Dakhni.org is an independent cultural project, not affiliated with any institution or government body. It exists to preserve and share the heritage of the Deccan, not to serve as an official or authoritative record.
